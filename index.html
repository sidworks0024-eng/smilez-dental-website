About the clinic

Two clinics, one standard of care.

Smilez Multi Speciality Dental Clinic was built around a simple idea: dentistry should feel calm, honest, and genuinely painless. Dr. Shailaja Patil leads a team of specialists across general dentistry, root canals, implants, orthodontics, and cosmetic smile design — using modern, digitally tracked treatment records so every visit picks up exactly where the last one left off.

Patients consistently mention the same things — a spotless, well-organised space, clear explanations before any procedure, and a team that never pushes treatment you don't need.
